# AD-190 Member previews remain readable

Keep compact card widths and wrap long names at identifier boundaries.
One long classifier previously widened every card and made overview headings
unreadable. Expanded members use native text size; large scopes pan rather than
shrink every member onto one screen.

Details retain complete names and evidence. Small call graphs keep complete
helper labels within the readability floor. Passing one overview cannot certify
all source-graph layouts.
