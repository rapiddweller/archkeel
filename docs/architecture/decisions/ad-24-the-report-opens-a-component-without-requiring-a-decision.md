# AD-24 The report opens a component without requiring a decision

Let reviewers open components and modules from the observation even when no inner contract exists.
Unruled edges are observed navigation, not permission or conformance. Only a scoped contract and its
checked evidence can decide a boundary.

Inner records and evaluation follow
[AD-34](ad-34-a-declared-inside-is-recorded-so-the-report-draws-it.md) and
[AD-110](ad-110-inside-rules-use-the-shared-evaluator.md).
