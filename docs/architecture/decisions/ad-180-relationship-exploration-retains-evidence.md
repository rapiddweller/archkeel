# AD-180 Relationship exploration retains evidence

Select relationships through the shared scene while retaining every site,
identity and Core assessment. Typed node/edge selection avoids ID collisions;
only edges with two visible endpoints get hit paths.

Drilled scopes retain connected outside modules and recorded imports. Outside cards
create no ownership, boundary or permission; they do not inflate local inventory.
A violation applies only to contributing evidence. Absence of a finding proves
no conformance. No second architecture model is persisted.
