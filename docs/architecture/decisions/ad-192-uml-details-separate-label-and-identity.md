# AD-192 UML details separate label and identity

Use the card label as the UML inspector heading, with full qualified identity
below it. Namespace break points and wrapped excerpts keep short names readable
without changing source text or indentation.

IDs, navigation and report data remain unchanged. Relationship headings retain
their meaning. Presentation labels cannot replace identity.
