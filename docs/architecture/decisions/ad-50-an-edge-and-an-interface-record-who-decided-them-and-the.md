# AD-50 An edge and an interface record who decided them, and the agent-decision count counts them

A component's `decided_by` attributes its public list and defaults its requirements; individual
requirements may override it. Count agent and total decisions from recorded declarations, including
inner levels, rather than maintain separate counters.

One attribution covers the whole public list. Attribution text neither authenticates authorship nor
identifies a particular edge. Proof: [test_decisions.py](../../../tests/test_decisions.py).
