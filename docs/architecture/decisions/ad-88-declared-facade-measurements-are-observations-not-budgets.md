# AD-88 Declared facade measurements are observations, not budgets

Measure declared facade exports and consumer-name crossings from existing facts. Measurements alone
are neither budgets nor proof of complete barrels. Explicit-name imports, proven stars and re-export
chains can establish usage; bare module imports cannot prove which names are used.

[AD-99](ad-99-facade-and-coupling-budgets-are-contract-ceilings.md) adds explicit ceilings without
another scanner or resolver. Proof: [test_self.py](../../../tests/test_self.py).
