BindingReads, SymbolReferences, TypeFanin, InsideSizes, OwnedLogic, DimensionDelta and RatchetObservations share that domain; they do not introduce private copies or borrow rule/metric verdicts.

The five analysis owners retain their algorithms, candidates and UNKNOWN invariants. Their actual producers return the canonical enum.
